- Tauri, `windows-sys`, Serde, and serde_json use MIT OR Apache-2.0. WindowsKeeper takes the MIT option where a choice is required.
- Other transitive runtime crates are under permissive MIT, Apache-2.0, BSD, ISC, Zlib, 0BSD, CC0, or Unlicense terms, as recorded in `src-tauri/Cargo.lock`.
- `r-efi` offers MIT OR Apache-2.0 OR LGPL-2.1-or-later; WindowsKeeper uses it under MIT.
- The system WebView2 runtime is used and is not bundled.
- The NSIS installer is generated by Tauri and installed per user.

`cssparser`, `cssparser-macros`, `dtoa-short`, `option-ext`, and `selectors` are unmodified transitive crates under MPL-2.0. Their source and licence files remain available from crates.io; the MPL applies at file level.
